# Primary-source boundary check (2026-10-13)

Opened both primary sources with web research tools today.

- Rozier–Terracol v4, Theorem 5.3: exactly 593 paradoxical sequences with n<=4614 and j<=92; no additional ones for 93<=j<=301993 or 4615<=n<=2.8e19. Corollary 5.4 verifies CST up to 2.8e19, not globally. Thus our J92/J300 finite reproductions are much weaker and not novelty.
  https://arxiv.org/html/2502.00948v4
- Angeltveit v1: recursive low-bit enumeration with descent, mod-9 preimage, path-merging and odd-even-even sieves verifies bounded convergence. These stopping/merging exclusions cannot be imported unchanged to enumerate all later paradoxical returns.
  https://arxiv.org/html/2602.10466v1

This comparison does not certify novelty of a new pruning rule or reproduce either paper's full calculations.
